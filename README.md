# 📊 Telecom Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the major challenges faced by the telecommunications industry. Retaining existing customers is essential for maintaining revenue, improving customer satisfaction, and achieving long-term business growth.

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn for a fictional telecommunications company providing phone and internet services to 7,043 customers across California.

The objective is to identify customer churn patterns, understand the factors influencing customer attrition, analyze revenue performance, and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention.

This project demonstrates my practical skills in data analytics, business intelligence, data visualization, and business problem-solving using Microsoft Power BI.

## 🎯 Business Problem Statement

The telecommunications company is experiencing customer attrition, which can negatively impact its revenue and business growth.

The management team wants to understand:

- What percentage of customers have discontinued their services?
- Which customer segments have the highest churn rates?
- Does contract type influence customer retention?
- Which phone and internet services are associated with higher churn?
- What are the major reasons behind customer churn?
- How does customer churn impact the company's revenue?
- Which customer segments should be prioritized for retention campaigns?

**Business Objective:** Analyze customer behavior and churn patterns to identify high-risk customer segments and recommend data-driven strategies to improve customer retention and revenue.

## 🔍 Key Business Questions

The project aims to answer the following questions:

1. What is the overall customer churn rate?
2. Which customer segments contribute most to churn?
3. Which contract types have higher customer attrition?
4. How do internet and phone services relate to customer churn?
5. What are the major reasons customers leave the company?
6. What is the relationship between customer tenure and churn?
7. How can the company improve customer retention?

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the company can consider the following strategies:

- **Improve Customer Retention:** Focus on customer segments experiencing higher churn.
- **Contract Optimization:** Develop retention strategies for customers on shorter-term contracts.
- **Customer Engagement:** Improve customer communication and service experience.
- **Targeted Offers:** Introduce personalized offers for customers who may be at risk of leaving.
- **Service Improvement:** Investigate service-related complaints and address recurring customer issues.
- **Revenue Protection:** Prioritize retention efforts for valuable customer segments.

*These recommendations are proposed business strategies and should be validated against the actual analytical findings.*
